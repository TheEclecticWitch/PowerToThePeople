Power to the People data

Public records of the U.S. Congress, gathered every six hours for the Power to the People app. Everything here is copied from official sources as published, and each record links to its source. Nothing is scored, ranked or summarized.

  • manifest.json: when the data was last updated, counts, and the list of sources
  • bills/{congress}/index.json, recent.json, {type}/{number}.json: bills and resolutions
  • votes/{house|senate}/{congress}-{session}.json, and one file per roll call vote
  • members/{bioguide id}.json: each member's roll-call positions and sponsored bills

The code that gathers the data is at github.com/TheEclecticWitch/PowerToThePeople (tools/gather).
